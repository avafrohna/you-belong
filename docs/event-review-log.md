## Arts programs awaiting calendar approval

**6. [Unzipped — introduction by Olga Cecilia Sanchez](https://digitalgym.org/movies/unzipped-4k-restoration/)**

A fashion documentary following designer Isaac Mizrahi as he prepares his 1994 collection. This screening includes an introduction by local designer Olga Cecilia Sanchez.

**7. [DGC Video: Hell’s Highway](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-video-hells-highway-the-true-story-of-highway-safety-films-2k-restoration/)**

A documentary about the shocking driver-education films made from 1959 to 1979 and the people behind them. Includes graphic footage of real crashes.

**8. [Zoé Presents: Memorex + Rexsexex y Más](https://digitalgym.org/movies/zoe-presents-memorex-rexsexex-y-mas/)**

A concert-film program featuring Mexican rock band Zoé and its large-scale Mexico City performances. Spanish with English subtitles.

**9. [DGC Screen Gems: The Addiction](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-screen-gems-the-addiction/)**

Abel Ferrara’s black-and-white vampire drama about a New York graduate student whose transformation becomes a study of addiction. Stars Lili Taylor and Christopher Walken.

**10. [DGC Screen Gems: Begotten](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-screen-gems-begotten-4k-restoration/)**

An experimental black-and-white horror film presenting an abstract creation myth about death and rebirth. Surreal and graphically violent.

**11. [DGC Screen Gems: The Fly](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-screen-gems-the-fly/)**

David Cronenberg’s science-fiction horror film about a scientist whose teleportation experiment merges him with a fly. Stars Jeff Goldblum and Geena Davis; graphic body horror.

**12. [DGC Screen Gems: Invasion of the Body Snatchers](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-screen-gems-invasion-of-the-body-snatchers/)**

The 1978 science-fiction thriller in which San Francisco residents are replaced by emotionless alien doubles. Stars Donald Sutherland, Leonard Nimoy, and Jeff Goldblum.

**13. [Film Geeks San Diego: Bone Tomahawk](https://digitalgym.org/movies/film-geeks-san-diego-bone-tomahawk/)**

A Western-horror rescue story starring Kurt Russell as a sheriff searching for kidnapped townspeople. Includes an exclusive filmmaker video introduction and very graphic violence.

**14. [DGC Video: Gandahar](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-video-gandahar-4k-restoration/)**

René Laloux’s French animated science-fiction fantasy about a peaceful society attacked by a mechanical army. Surreal creatures, strange technology, and a quest to save the civilization.

**15. [Taxi Driver — film and discussion](https://digitalgym.org/movies/burke-revival-film-and-discussion-series-taxi-driver/)**

Martin Scorsese’s drama about an isolated New York taxi driver descending into obsession and violence. Followed by a guided discussion with Rev. Scott Young through the Burke film series.

**16. [DGC Video: The Legend of the Stardust Brothers](https://digitalgym.org/movies/dgc-video-the-legend-of-the-stardust-brothers/)**

A Japanese cult musical about a punk rocker and a new-wave singer transformed into a pop duo by a shady music mogul. A surreal satire of fame and the music industry.

**17. [Film Geeks San Diego: Ride the High Country](https://digitalgym.org/movies/film-geeks-san-diego-ride-the-high-country/)**

Sam Peckinpah’s classic Western about an aging former soldier escorting gold while his partner plots a betrayal. A story of loyalty, honor, and growing older.

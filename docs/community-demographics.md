# Community statistics used on Home and Our Story

Reviewed September 25, 2026. Source of truth: `src/data/community-facts.js`.

The originally supplied HHSA demographics page contains an undated 21.5% immigrant figure, a 68-language count, and a forecast for 2020. These were not used as current statistics:
https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa/statistics_demographics.html

Use the County of San Diego HHSA, Public Health Services, Community Health Statistics Unit **2020–2024 City Demographic Profiles**, published March 2026:
https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/dam/sdc/hhsa/programs/phs/CHS/2024%20City%20Demographic%20Profiles.pdf

Despite the report's title, it includes a separately labelled **San Diego County** row. Both website statistics use that countywide row, not the City of San Diego row.

- **22.8% foreign-born:** printed page 44 (PDF page 47), “Foreign-Born in the Population,” ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates, table DP02. County estimate: 749,735 people. The Census definition is people who were not U.S. citizens or U.S. nationals at birth; do not reinterpret this as a refugee count or current citizenship status.
- **37.3% speaking a language other than English at home, age 5+:** printed page 14 (PDF page 17), “Language Spoken at Home for the Population 5+ Years and Ability to Speak English,” same ACS period and table. Calculated as 100% minus the county's 62.7% speaking English only. This is a rounded estimate for people age five and older, not households, all ages, or a measure of English proficiency. The website keeps the age qualifier next to the statistic.

Visible source notes on both pages state the county geography, 2020–2024 estimate period, publication date, and link to the report. Do not present these as a 2026 census count or infer a current language count from the old page. The narrative emphasizes cultures, languages, traditions, and belonging while preserving the founder's human-rights, privacy, free-expression, peace, and community-business mission.
